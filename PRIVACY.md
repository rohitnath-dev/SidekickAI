## 2. Information From Connected Services

SidekickAI can connect with third-party services that you choose to authorize.

The information available to SidekickAI depends on the service, permissions you grant, and features you use.

### 2.1 Google and Gmail

When you connect a Google account, SidekickAI may access information from Gmail and, where enabled, Google Calendar through Google's authorization system.

Depending on the permissions granted, this may include:

- Gmail messages and related message information.
- Email content required to provide communication-analysis features.
- Information required to generate summaries and briefings.
- Information required to identify priorities, deadlines, and action items.
- Information required to prepare draft replies.
- Google Calendar events and related schedule information where Calendar access is enabled.

SidekickAI uses this information to provide the features you have enabled, including communication analysis, executive briefings, prioritization, and draft generation.

Google authorization is controlled through Google's OAuth system. You can revoke SidekickAI's access from your Google Account settings.

### 2.2 Google OAuth Credentials

When you authorize Google integrations, SidekickAI may receive OAuth credentials that allow the Service to maintain the authorized connection.

These credentials are stored and handled as authentication secrets and are used only to provide the connected functionality.

### 2.3 WhatsApp Business

Where WhatsApp Business integration is supported, SidekickAI may process information made available through the authorized WhatsApp Business Platform connection.

This may include:

- WhatsApp Business Account information.
- Connected business phone-number information.
- Customer messages and related communication data available through the authorized integration.

The WhatsApp integration is intended for supported WhatsApp Business accounts and does not provide SidekickAI with access to personal WhatsApp profiles or unrelated private chats.

### 2.4 Other Integrations

SidekickAI may support additional integrations over time.

When you connect another service, the information accessible to SidekickAI will depend on the permissions granted and the functionality provided by that integration.
